Hydrology with AI and
Remote Sensing

We investigate the impact of climate changes on regional to the global scale water cycle with microwave remote sensing, land surface models, and AI/machine learning technics (i.e., foundation model).

Our goal is to understand how water moves across the land surface and how this movement relates to broader scale hydrological and climate behavior. By applying microwave remote sensing and AI techniques in the context of the climate crisis, we aim to improve the accuracy of natural disaster forecasts, water resource predictions, and numerical weather prediction (NWP).
